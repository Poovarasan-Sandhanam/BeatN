Common questions

Why does BeatN download 1.2 GB the first time?

BeatN understands your voice entirely on your phone, which needs a speech model and a language model. They are downloaded once — Wi-Fi is best — and after that BeatN works offline. The download is checked for damage before it is used.

Does anything I say leave my phone?

No. Recording, transcription and reflections all happen on the device. There is no account and no upload. See the privacy policy.

An entry says "Needs attention".

Its processing did not finish. Your recording is safe — open the entry and tap Try again.

My recording sounds quiet, or I hear nothing on playback.

Recordings are levelled automatically when you save them. If playback is silent, turn up the volume; BeatN plays through the loudspeaker even with the silent switch on.

What happens if I lock my phone while recording?

The recording pauses. When you come back, tap the microphone to keep going, or the stop button to save. If the phone closes BeatN while paused, what you said is still saved and appears in your journal next time.

How do I unlock BeatN?

With Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. BeatN locks again after 30 seconds in the background.

How do I delete everything?

Deleting an entry removes it from your journal. To erase all of BeatN's data, delete the app from your phone.

Is BeatN a substitute for professional help?

No. BeatN is a journal, not a therapist. If you are struggling, please contact a professional or a local support service.
