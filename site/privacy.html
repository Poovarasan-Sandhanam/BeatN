BeatN

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: everything you record in BeatN stays on your phone. BeatN has no account, no servers that receive your journal, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see anything you record.

What BeatN handles, and where it stays

To work as a journal, BeatN creates and stores the following on your device only:

  • your voice recordings;
  • transcripts of what you said;
  • reflections, suggestions, moods and topics generated from your entries;
  • measurements of how you sounded (such as loudness, pitch and pace), used to describe the energy in your voice;
  • the date and length of each entry.

All of this is produced on your phone by speech and language models that run on the device. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

How it is protected

  • Your journal is stored in an encrypted database. Its key is created on your phone and kept in the system's secure keychain.
  • On iPhone, BeatN's files — including recordings — use the strongest iOS file protection: they cannot be read while your phone is locked.
  • BeatN can be opened only after Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Biometric checks are performed by your phone's operating system; BeatN never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • When you switch apps, BeatN hides its contents so they do not appear in the app switcher.

What BeatN does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • No sale or sharing of data — there is no data to share.
  • No use of your entries to train any model.

The one-time model download

The first time you open BeatN, it downloads its speech and language models (about 1.2 GB) from Hugging Face. As with any download, Hugging Face receives standard connection information such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Nothing from your journal is ever sent; the models are downloaded, not your data uploaded. After this, BeatN works fully offline.

Permissions

  • Microphone — used only while you are recording an entry.
  • Face ID — used only to unlock your journal.

Backups

If you back up your phone (for example with iCloud Backup), your encrypted journal may be included in that backup, so it can be restored to a new phone. Backups are governed by your device and account settings, not by BeatN. On Android, BeatN opts out of automatic app backup.

Deleting your data

Deleting an entry removes it from your journal immediately. To erase everything BeatN has stored — all entries, recordings and downloaded models — delete the app from your phone.

Reflections are not advice

Moods, reflections and suggestions are generated automatically and can be wrong. BeatN is a journal, not a therapist, and nothing in it is medical or psychological advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to a professional or a local support service.

Children

BeatN is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
