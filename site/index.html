BeatN

Your thoughts, kept private.

BeatN is a voice journal. Talk for a minute; it writes down what you said, notices how you sounded, and reflects it back — entirely on your phone.

  • Just talk. No typing, no blank page.
  • Understood on your phone. Transcripts, moods and reflections are made on the device, offline.
  • Never uploaded. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Your journal is encrypted and locked with Face ID.
